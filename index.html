Our Story

Our Beginning

High School Sweethearts

Diana and Christian’s love story began in the hallways of high school when Christian first asked Diana to prom. Since then they have travelled the world, developed new interests, and moved to new places but always did it together as a team. From the tiny question of “Join Me at Prom?” to the big question of “Will You Marry Me?” their relationship has blossomed into one of commitment, love, and laughter.

Together is a beautiful place to be.

The Proposal

A Swiss Yes

In March 2025, while traveling through the valleys of the snowcapped mountains in Switzerland, Christian popped the question in the picturesque village of Lucerne. With a hop of excitement and a tearful “Yes!” their love was immortalized in a ring that promised a loving future together forever.

The Civil Ceremony

A Civil in Sarasota

Four months after Switzerland, Diana and Christian cemented their love in Sarasota’s historic courthouse. With a heartfelt “I do” followed by hugs and tears with family, their love was sealed and ready for Antigua.

Planning Help

Travel

Fly into La Aurora International Airport (GUA) in Guatemala City, then plan approximately 1 to 1.5 hours for the drive to Antigua, depending on traffic and arrival time.

Antigua, Guatemala

Flights

Fly into Guatemala City via La Aurora International Airport (GUA). We recommend arriving at least one day early to enjoy Antigua.

3–6 hr flight time Direct or 1 stop Best arrival: Thu–Fri

July Travel Insight

July is peak travel season. Morning arrivals are typically smoother, and brief afternoon showers are common but pass quickly. This weekend may see increased traffic due to a local Patron Saint celebration.

Airport Code: GUA Explore Flights

Transportation

We recommend arranging a private driver or shuttle from La Aurora International Airport (GUA) to Antigua. This is the most comfortable and reliable option. Uber is also widely used within Guatemala City and Antigua.

1–1.5 hour drive Private or shared shuttle

Travel Time & Traffic

Off peak: Approximately 1 to 1.5 hours
Rush hour (4:00–7:30 PM): Expect 2 to 3 plus hours
Fridays and Sundays: Longer travel delays are common

Arrival Tip

Daytime arrivals are easiest. Evening arrivals are fine when transportation is arranged in advance, just plan for traffic.

Things to Do

Antigua blends history, culture, and natural beauty. From cobblestone streets to scenic viewpoints and local cuisine, the city is best explored at a relaxed pace.

July 25th also marks Antigua’s annual Patron Saint celebration in honor of Saint James (Santiago Apóstol). The city comes alive with local festivities, parades, music, and cultural events throughout the historic center.

No. This celebration welcomes guests of all ages. We look forward to celebrating with your entire family.

When is the RSVP deadline? Coming soon

Kindly submit your RSVP by May 15. This helps us finalize guest counts and make the necessary arrangements.

What should I wear? Dress code

Formal cocktail attire is requested. Antigua evenings can be cool after sunset, so a light layer is recommended. For men, suits or blazers with tailored pants, a button down, and tie are encouraged. For women, mid to floor length dresses in fabrics such as silk, chiffon, or satin are recommended. Please avoid white and shades of raspberry. Bring out your summer colors.

How do I get to Antigua? Transportation

Fly into La Aurora International Airport (GUA) in Guatemala City, then plan about a 1–1.5 hour drive to Antigua. We recommend arranging a private driver or shuttle in advance.

Will there be food and drinks? Yes

Yes. Guests will enjoy a full dinner with drinks and dessert throughout the evening. We will do our best to accommodate all dietary restrictions. If there are any allergies, please include when submitting your RSVP
